Created as cover artwork for Minutes, this piece translates the laid-back energy of the music into a visual road-trip scene. The artwork places the main artists inside a cruising car, surrounded by palm trees and a warm, carefree environment, giving the cover a relaxed and cinematic feel.

I developed the illustration from the concept through the final artwork, focusing on expressive characters, composition, atmosphere, and a strong visual identity that works as a music cover.
